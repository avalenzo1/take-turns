How to Play

In this game teams compete against each other to find who can draw the best and the fastest. There are three rounds and teams of up to four players. For each round, each team is given a word they have to draw. Sounds like regular old charades, right? Wrong! In your given round, you only get a few seconds to draw the word and so does everyone else in your team. In order to win, your team must work together in drawing the word without messing up.

Rules

  • Players cannot draw the word out itself
  • Each team player must contribute otherwise penalties will incur

Warning

This game is under early development, so bugs are expected. Any bug reports sent to this repo will be greatly appreciated.
